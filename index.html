I am an M.S. candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, Seoul, Korea, where I am a member of the Machine Intelligence Lab (MILAB) advised by Prof. Kyomin Jung. My research interest is in the field of Natural Language Processing, especially about (i) AI Safety and Instruction Hierarchy of Large Language Models, (ii) Machine Unlearning, and (iii) Multimodal Learning and Evaluation.

I am broadly interested in understanding how language models behave when instructions, languages, or modalities come into conflict, and in building evaluation methods that expose such failures. Feel free to reach out if you would like to chat about any of these topics.
